How this was made

Speaker turns come from the Deepgram transcript, merged by voice and gap as described in the repository README; the parts of the hearing and who sat on each are in panels.json. Times are seconds from the start of the Council's recording, not the clock time of the hearing.

Speaker names come from self-introductions, the chair calling on people, and voice clusters. Names were assigned by AI agents working from these sources, at BetaNYC's direction. Elected officials were checked against Legistar and NYS Open Legislation. Other names were not individually verified by a person.

Talk time is approximate. 56 of the 248 turns hold two speakers: a question and the start of its answer, for example, where the diarization did not split them. Those turns count toward the person named on the turn.

Built by scripts/build_panels.py from panels.json, transcript/turns.csv and speakers.csv. This is a machine transcript, not an official record; check the video before quoting anyone.
