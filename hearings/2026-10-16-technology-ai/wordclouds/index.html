How this was made

“Distinctive” means a phrase a group said much more often than all other speakers, after allowing for how much each side talked, scored with the weighted log-odds test in Monroe, Colaresi and Quinn (2008), “Fightin' Words: Lexical Feature Selection and Evaluation for Identifying the Content of Political Conflict,” Political Analysis 16(4). Counts come from the clean verbatim transcript. Groups come from AI-assigned speaker labels. 56 turns at this hearing hold two voices; their words count toward the person named on the turn, so a cloud can include some words said by another speaker. This is a machine transcript; check the video before quoting anyone. Each page explains the details. Code: scripts/build_wordclouds.py.

Common words, speakers' names, “New York”, “city”, “AI” and hearing boilerplate are left out, using the word lists made for the 2026-10-05 hearing, which include filler an AI assistant proposed cutting from that hearing's lists. This hearing's lists were not reviewed by hand. Word lists: scripts/meta/wordclouds.json; this hearing's groups: scripts/meta/2026-10-16-technology-ai-wordclouds.json.
