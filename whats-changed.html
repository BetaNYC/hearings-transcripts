What's changed

This page lists the changes to BetaNYC's NYC Council hearing transcripts that a reader would notice: new hearings, new pages, and fixes to names or text, plus corrections when we published something wrong. Newest first.

  • History begins: 2026-10-06, the day the first hearing was published. Every change since then is listed.
  • Data refresh: none on a schedule. BetaNYC adds a hearing by hand, and a change goes live on the website a few minutes after it is merged on GitHub.

These are machine transcripts, not an official record of the Council. To report a wrong name, a wrong word or a missing document, use the "Open an issue on GitHub" link at the bottom of any page, or open an issue.

Clearer notes on talk time and two-voice turns

October 19, 2026

Tags: Site

The pages now explain that talk time includes short pauses, and that some turns hold two voices, which are credited to the person named on the turn. For the October 16 hearing they give the count of such turns. Speakers who talked for under a minute now show seconds. The October 16 hearing gained its full-quality audio and an Internet Archive copy of the video, and the October 5 archive copy is marked playable.
